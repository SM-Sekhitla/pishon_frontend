2️⃣ How to Advertise on GOOGLE (Step-by-Step)

This is where leads will come from 💰

A. Google Search Ads (BEST option)

Recruitment is high-intent, so Google Search Ads work very well.

Example searches people make:

“executive search firms south africa”

“recruitment agencies sandton”

“headhunting companies johannesburg”

“senior management recruitment”

How it works:

Create Google Ads account

Choose Search Campaign

Target South Africa (or Gauteng only)

Bid on keywords

Ads show at top of Google results

Example Ad Copy:

Headline:
Executive Search Firm in South Africa

Description:
Specialist executive recruitment & leadership advisory. Trusted by top organizations. Book a consultation today.

B. Landing Page (DO NOT send ads to homepage)

This increases conversions massively.
